# Captions

One line per picture in this folder. Every screen shows the fictional
sample family (Mira and Kabir, `content.sample/`).

| File | Caption |
|---|---|
| `01-day.png` | The day board. Everything the two of them need for today and tomorrow, and nothing else. |
| `02-kid-card.png` | A kid's own card. Tap a chore to claim it; a parent approves it. |
| `03-approvals.png` | The parent side is a small settings app. Approve, decline, decide, done. |
| `04-rewards.png` | Points buy ordinary things. The Roblox rows stay off until the policy plane can enforce them. |
| `05-meal-picks.png` | Kids pick the week's meals for free. When they disagree, the parents decide. |
| `06-night.png` | After ten the wall goes quiet: the time, tomorrow's weather, tomorrow's plan, and what to pack. |
| `07-frame.png` | At rest it is a photo frame. A tap brings the day back; nothing under the tap fires. |
| `08-parent-phone.png` | The same parent screen on a phone, from the sofa. |
| `09-day-portrait.png` | The day board with the iPad stood upright. |
| `10-month.png` | The month: school days, festivals, each kid's days, and the lunar calendar. |
